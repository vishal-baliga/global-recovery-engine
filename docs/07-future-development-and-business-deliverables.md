# Global Recovery Engine — Future Development & Business Deliverables

**Date:** September 6, 2026  
**Version:** 0.1 — scope register accompanying the proposed pilot brief  
**Owner:** Vishal  
**Companion:** `06-pilot-mvp-brief.md`

This register preserves the broader offering while the pilot covers domestic U.S. personal/friend loans and India–U.S. B2B recovery. It is an unprioritized record of future candidates, not a release schedule or a commitment to implement every possible business model. IDs provide traceability; their order is not priority.

“Future” refers to depth or reach beyond the pilot. A capability delivered through reviewed manual work or a partner is still included in the pilot. Essential service responsibility, data protection, financial correctness, lawful execution, and usable customer outcomes cannot be moved here merely because implementation is unfinished.

## 4. Strategic boundaries kept on record

These exclusions remain visible rather than disappearing from the roadmap discussion. They are **not promised future deliverables**; adopting one requires an explicit business-model decision and reassessment of responsibilities, capital, and positioning.

| ID | Boundary or separate-company option |
| --- | --- |
| **X01** | GRE purchases, owns, finances, securitizes, or underwrites debt; originates loans; offers a lending marketplace or general credit-scoring product. |
