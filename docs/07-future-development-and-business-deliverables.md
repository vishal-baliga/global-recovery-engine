## 1. Scope and disposition rules

The sole planned release is the Pilot: **GRE Foundation + GRE Trade & Receivables**, including the required Network & Control subset. Private Resolution, Consumer Credit Infrastructure, and separately sold Network functionality are future development. The selected Trade corridor and creditor-side recurring buyer remain hypotheses, with live activation governed by [13](13-pilot-release-and-assurance.md).

This register retains every original F-P01–F-P16, F-B01–F-B07 and X01–X05 identifier. Their meaning is reconciled below; no old ID implies that the former personal-loan track or GRE-operated collection remains in the Pilot. New F-P17–F-P19 make the deferred families/packaging explicit.

**Split** means the stated minimum remains in the Pilot and additional depth is deferred. **Deferred candidate** means no live Pilot commitment. **Excluded** means a boundary, not a planned feature. Necessary correctness, support, security, qualified review and usable customer outcomes remain Pilot requirements even if a manual or provider step supplies them. Every such step needs an owner and measured effort.

## 4. Future-family needs and reopening gates

**Private Resolution (F-P17)** preserves the original domestic U.S. personal/friend-loan need, with a revised voluntary role. A requesting participant may reconstruct advances, terms, messages, repayments and disagreement privately without the other person enrolling. Missing terms remain unknown; a transfer alone does not prove a loan. “Gift,” cash-repayment, hardship, wrong-person, abuse and no-evidence branches must stay visible. Participation or payment does not certify debt validity.

If another person voluntarily joins, verify identity and exact agreement authority, maintain separate private contexts, disclose only approved material, and support exact proposals, acceptance, withdrawal and safe exit. A sponsor/payer gets no automatic access to the other participant's strategy, hardship or safety reports. No response ends in a useful private assessment/export, not coerced enrollment or repeated pursuit. Reopen only with a bounded state/activity review, terms, abuse and invitation controls, independent support/complaints, qualified routes and credible demand/economics. Loan origination, collection pressure, adjudication and GRE funds remain excluded.

**Consumer Credit (F-P18)** preserves continuity when account, creditor, servicer, provider or borrower location changes. Retain original account/rights, current location, disputes, hardship/protection holds, preferences, complaints, prior contact, balances and authority separately. A U.S.-origin account with an India-resident borrower requires its own directional analysis; location alone does not settle applicable roles or rules. Reopening needs a regulated design partner, qualified current corridor/actor/activity review, lender/provider responsibilities, lawful data access/storage/transfer topology, security/vendor assurance and credible support economics. Consumer reports and bureau furnishing remain excluded from the baseline. Neither shared schemas nor an enterprise edition activate consumer operations.

**Standalone Network (F-P19)** can reuse Trade-proven contracts if buyers pay for multi-provider continuity independently of preparation. Require measurable provider intake/reporting benefit, transparency, permitted data use and truthful correction/recall/switch evidence. Broader branding cannot imply verified providers or coverage that do not exist.

## 5. Strategic boundaries

These identifiers are **excluded**, not promised future deliverables. X01–X04 require an explicit business-model decision before reconsideration. X05 records prohibited practices.

| ID | Boundary |
|---|---|
| **X01** | GRE buys, owns, finances, securitizes or underwrites debt; originates loans; operates a lending marketplace or general credit-scoring business. |
| **X02** | GRE holds, pools, escrows or directly transmits recovered funds, or becomes a general remittance/payment institution. Authorized external payment integrations remain possible. |
| **X03** | GRE becomes the principal collection agency under one universal contract, provides legal representation/adjudication, or owns a field/enforcement workforce. A qualified-prime or ring-fenced managed-execution option would require a separate role, licensing, staffing, insurance and economics decision. |
| **X04** | A broad accounting/order-to-cash suite, standalone general compliance SaaS, proprietary foundation model, telephony/dialer infrastructure or paid-listing marketplace becomes the core product. |
| **X05** | Harassment, threats, public shaming, coercive employer/social contact, impersonation, hidden referral bias/pay-to-rank, unauthorized acts, fabricated evidence/terms, opaque unvalidated scores, or false impartiality while acting for one side. |
